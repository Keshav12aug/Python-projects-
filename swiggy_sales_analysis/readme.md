🍔 Swiggy Sales Analysis

A comprehensive data analysis project exploring Swiggy food delivery sales data using Python. This project focuses on extracting business insights, measuring key performance indicators (KPIs), and visualizing customer ordering patterns through interactive and static visualizations.

📌 Project Overview

The objective of this project is to analyze Swiggy sales data and answer important business questions such as:

How much revenue was generated?
What is the average customer rating?
Which cities contribute the most sales?
How do sales vary over time?
What is the revenue contribution of Veg vs Non-Veg food items?
How does quarterly business performance compare?

The analysis helps identify sales trends, customer preferences, and potential areas for business growth.

📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The project calculates:

💰 Total Sales

Overall revenue generated from all orders.

⭐ Average Rating

Average customer satisfaction score.

🛒 Average Order Value

Average revenue earned per order.

📝 Total Rating Count

Total number of customer ratings received.

📦 Total Orders

Total orders recorded in the dataset.

📈 Visualizations
1. Monthly Sales Trend

Analyzes revenue growth and seasonal sales patterns across different months.

2. Daily Revenue Trend

Shows revenue distribution across weekdays to identify high-performing days.

3. Veg vs Non-Veg Revenue Contribution

Classifies dishes into Veg and Non-Veg categories and compares their revenue share using an interactive donut chart.

4. Quarterly Performance Summary

Provides a quarterly breakdown of:

Total Sales
Average Rating
Total Orders
5. Top 5 Cities by Sales

Highlights the highest revenue-generating cities using a horizontal bar chart.
